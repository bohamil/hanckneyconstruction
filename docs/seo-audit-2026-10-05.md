## Official guidance used

- [Google SEO Starter Guide](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/seo-starter-guide): useful content, clear titles/descriptions, descriptive internal links and images; no automatic ranking guarantee.
- [Google structured-data policies](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/sd-policies): factual visible information and entity links; markup alone does not guarantee a search feature.
- [LocalBusiness structured data](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/local-business): completeness/required fields; don't manufacture address or review data to satisfy eligibility.
- [Google spam policies](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies): avoid keyword stuffing and low-value doorway/location expansion.
- [WCAG text contrast](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/contrast-minimum.html) and [label in name](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/label-in-name.html): readable action text and accessible names containing visible link text.
- [Google Business Profile local ranking guidance](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7091): accurate business information and local relevance/distance/prominence.

Existing FAQ markup was not expanded. Google's former FAQ feature documentation currently redirects to its updates page, so this audit makes no FAQ rich-result eligibility or traffic promise.
