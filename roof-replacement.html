Straight answers

Roof replacement questions, answered plainly.

How long does a roof replacement take?

Most single-family homes in our area go from tear-off to finished roof in one to two days, weather permitting. A recent Chariton farmhouse went from tear-off to dried-in in a single day.

Will my homeowners insurance pay for a new roof?

If the damage came from a covered storm event like hail or wind, it often does. We photograph and document the damage, and we can walk the roof with your adjuster. Most policies have a filing window after a storm, so get an inspection sooner rather than later.

Should I repair, rejuvenate, or replace my roof?

It depends on the age of the shingles, the condition of the decking, and how the roof has weathered. We inspect for free, show you photos of what we find, and tell you plainly which option makes sense - including when a repair or Fresh Roof rejuvenation can put off a full replacement for years.

What does the inspection cost?

Nothing. The inspection is free, there is no obligation, and you do not need to climb up there with us. You get photos and plain-English findings either way.

Should I choose asphalt shingles or metal?

Architectural asphalt shingles cost less up front and are easy to repair. Metal costs more but lasts longer and handles Iowa wind and hail well. We install both and will give you a straight comparison for your house instead of pushing one.

What about cleanup and my yard?

Tear-off debris goes in the trailer, not your lawn. We run a magnet sweep for nails around the house and driveway before we leave the jobsite.
